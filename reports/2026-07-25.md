# Software Shift Tracker — 2026-07-25

**Window:** 2026-06-13 → 2026-07-25.

## What's new this week

- **Salesforce acquires Fin (Intercom) for $3.6B (June 15)** — 30K AI customers, 76% auto-resolution rate folded into Agentforce. Independent outcome-pricing market collapses into one player.
- **ServiceNow AI ACV crosses $1B for first time (Q2, July 22)** — 40% sequential QoQ growth; 89 deals with $1M+ AI ACV; 9x agentic production deployments in nine months.
- **Gartner: $234B enterprise SaaS spend at risk from agentic AI (July 1)** — "Agentic arbitrage" coined; 20% of enterprise app software at risk of displacement by 2030.
- **Sierra acquires Takeoff + launches Horizon Agent Platform (July 23)** — Long-horizon multi-day/week agent workflows; SoftBank Japan exclusive (July 14); fourth acquisition of 2026.
- **Harvey crosses $300M ARR (June 2026), acquires Benchmark (July 16)** — $100M net-new ARR in Q2 alone; YC-backed asset-management decision platform adds $2T AUM foothold.
- **Inference infrastructure raises $3.8B in four weeks** — Fireworks AI $17.5B (July 16), Baseten $13B (~June 22), Together AI $8.3B (July 1). Picks-and-shovels layer is now the capital-intensity center of AI.
- **M365 price increase live July 1** — 8–43% depending on plan; Copilot bundled into all tiers with no opt-out; "AI tax" framing taking hold across enterprise IT.

- **Intercom Fin** → Salesforce acquiring for $3.6B (definitive June 15); 30K AI customers, $0.99/resolution unchanged through close (expected Q4 FY27). Removing as independent signal; tracked under Salesforce.
- **Sierra** → SoftBank Japan exclusive distribution (July 14); Takeoff acquisition + Horizon long-horizon agent platform (July 23); Agent OS 2.0 + Agent Data Platform; 40%+ of Fortune 50. Category leader pulling away from peers.
- **Harvey** → $300M ARR June 2026 (was $190M January); Benchmark acquisition July 16 expands into asset management; Singapore + Chicago offices opened. M&A workflow + asset management is the emerging double moat.

- **ServiceNow** → AI ACV $1B+ (Q2 July 22, first time ever); net new AI ACV +40% QoQ; 89 deals with $1M+ AI ACV; raised FY26 subscription revenue guide to $15.77B.
- **Cognition/Devin** → Two acquisitions in three days (TierZero for incident automation July 20; Poke messaging agent ~$100M+ July 23); Japan expansion live; $10M AI Productivity Guarantee launched. Rapidly expanding beyond pure coding agent.
- **11x.ai** → CEO Prabhav Jain on CNBC July 10: *"I don't think AI SDRs work, right? I don't think they work in the current form."* Pivoting narrative; company still operational.
- **Lovable** → $500M ARR (June 9); in talks to raise $300M at $13.2B valuation (July 8); enterprise growth 340% YoY despite BOLA crisis. Vibe-coding category leader.
- **Vapi** → xAI no-code Voice Agent Builder launch (July 1, $0.05/min); 21 new multilingual voices across 25+ languages (July 6). xAI ecosystem lock-in deepening.
- **Workday** → Aneel Bhusri replaced Carl Eschenbach as CEO (announced May). Paradox and Flowise acquisitions completed. >4,000 clients using at least one agent (doubled QoQ). Agent Passport still early access.
- **Google Antigravity** → Gemini CLI sunset completed June 18; 295:6 downvote ratio on GitHub announcement thread; Gemini 3.6 Flash added July 21. No enterprise adoption numbers disclosed.
- **"Ode with Anthropic"** → JV formally launched July 15; Baker Tilly first named client July 20. AI services implementation thesis now live alongside OpenAI DeployCo.
