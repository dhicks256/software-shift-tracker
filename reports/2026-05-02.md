## Executive Summary

The popular "SaaS is dead, AI is eating it" narrative is directionally interesting and factually overstated. Public SaaS revenue is still growing 10-20%, and **zero major public SaaS company has filed a disclosure attributing seat or revenue contraction to AI substitution**. What is real: per-seat pricing fell from 21% to 15% of SaaS companies in 12 months while hybrid pricing surged from 27% to 41%; AaaS revenue exists but is small, pilot-heavy, and partly vendor-fabricated; Klarna publicly walked back its AI-only customer service in 2025. Four product shapes are credibly emerging — Agent-as-a-Service (where the agent is the worker), Outcome-Driven Apps (priced per resolved unit), Embedded AI Analytics (NL-over-semantic-layer inside other products), and Hyper-Personalized Micro-SaaS (solo/micro-team products with workflow or audience moats). For SMBs and solopreneurs the durable plays are vertical-AI-native, agency-shaped (productized services + AI), or platform-distributed; the wrapper graveyard is large and growing. Treat any AaaS pitch where ARR isn't disclosed in a footnote with crypto-grade skepticism.

## 1. Market Context — SaaS Under AI Pressure

### Categories visibly repricing or contracting

- **Customer-support SaaS** — repricing from seats to per-resolution. Salesforce cut ~4,000 customer-service roles in Sep 2025 and Benioff said AI agents now handle ~50% of interactions. Zendesk relaunched as the "Resolution Platform" at Relate 2025: $1.50/automated resolution committed, $2.00 PAYG.
- **Education / homework-help** — Chegg laid off ~45% of staff in 2025, explicitly citing student migration to ChatGPT.
- **HR / back-office of record** — Workday cut ~8.5% of staff early 2025; a16z's "Workday's Last Workday?" essay names the system-of-record UI layer as the part being unbundled.
- **SDR / sales-engagement seats** — directly attacked by HubSpot Breeze Prospecting Agent ($1/qualified lead) and Agentforce SDR ($2/conversation).
- **Dev tools / RPA / low-code** — repackaged into agents. Atlassian Rovo went GA Apr 9 2025, included free in paid Cloud plans (deflationary). Rovo Dev at $20/dev/month.
- **BI dashboards** — squeezed from above (Snowflake Cortex / NL-over-warehouse) and below (vertical AI analytics).

### Why SaaS is the exposed surface (structural)

It is **(a) + (b) + (c)** combined:

- **(a) The per-seat model breaks** when an agent does work an FTE used to do. Tomasz Tunguz: "value no longer scales with human users." Per-seat dropped 21% → 15% of SaaS companies in 12 months; hybrid surged 27% → 41% (Monetizely / SaaStr 2025).
- **(b) Thin-wrapper risk is real.** a16z's "Why the World Still Runs on SAP" / "Workday's Last Workday?" framing: systems of record endure but the **interface/automation layer** becomes the new contested front. Jamin Ball's Dec 19 2025 "The Front Door to the Systems of Record" names it explicitly.
- **(c) Buyer is migrating to the CFO buying outcomes.** Bessemer's thesis: vertical AI is 10× the TAM of vertical SaaS because it taps the ~13% of US GDP spent on labor, not the ~1% spent on IT. CFOs' AI budget allocation is now ~25% explicitly tagged for agents.

### Valuations (2025-2026 actual data)

- **BVP Emerging Cloud Index:** ~6× ARR Oct 2025 (vs 17× peak 2021). Real compression but closer to 2017-2018 normalization than extinction.
- **BVP Cloud 100 (private):** median 20× ARR in 2025 — 41% decline from 2023 peak.
- **Meritech Software Pulse (Feb 25 2025):** public software median EV/Revenue ~6× by July; only 6 pure-play software IPOs in 2025 vs 27 in 2021. M&A volume hit $587B — exits moving from IPO to acquisition.
- **Jamin Ball (Clouded Judgement):** "high-growth bucket has seen multiple expansion this year, while the mid-growth bucket has seen steady contraction" — a bifurcated market where AI-native compounders re-rate up and "static SaaS" gets de-rated.

**Operator read:** revenue is fine, multiples compressed, and pricing models are visibly moving. The 10-K disclosures are 2-3× behind the Twitter narrative.

## 2. Agent-as-a-Service (AaaS)

### Definition (sharp)

| | AI-Powered SaaS | Agent-as-a-Service |
|---|---|---|
| What's sold | Software with AI features | The agent IS the worker |
| Pricing | Per seat | Per outcome / per task / per FTE-equivalent |
| Autonomy | Single-turn assist | Plan-execute-verify over hours/days |
| Budget line | Software (IT) | Labor (OpEx) |
| Operator test | "Makes my SDRs 30% faster" | "Replaces 3 SDRs at $80K each" |

Bret Taylor (Sierra) frames it explicitly as "software you hire" — a deliberate move into the labor P&L, not the IT line.

### Named companies + pricing

- **Cognition (Devin)** — autonomous SWE on bounded tasks. Team plans from ~$500/mo + ACU consumption (hybrid). Customers: Goldman Sachs, MongoDB, Ramp, Nubank. Pricing collapsed from $500/mo to $20/mo "team" tier in late 2024 — a tell. Independent SWE-Bench testing put completion at ~13-15% of real tasks. Cognition acquired Windsurf assets in 2025.
- **Sierra (Bret Taylor)** — CX agents. Outcome pricing ~$1.50/resolution; floor near $150K/yr. Logos: SiriusXM, Sonos, ADT, WeightWatchers, Ramp, Casper. WeightWatchers reports ~70% of sessions handled with 4.6/5 CSAT. Reported ~$50-60M ARR mid-2025; rumored $10B valuation discussions. ARR-to-valuation ratio is 150-200× — historically unprecedented.
- **Decagon** — enterprise CX, median ACV ~$400K. Logos: Eventbrite, Bilt, Notion, Duolingo, Substack, Rippling, Hertz, ClassPass. Reported ~$30-40M ARR in 2025, ~$1.5B valuation. **Concentration risk:** revenue heavily in 4-5 anchor customers.
- **11x.ai** — AI SDR ("Alice") and voice agent ("Jordan"). The cautionary tale of the cohort. Claimed ~$10M ARR end of 2024; *TechCrunch* and *The Information* (Mar-Apr 2025) reported actual net revenue closer to $2-3M after refunds and unpaid pilots. Two layoff rounds Q1-Q2 2025 (~25-30% of staff). Investors marked the round down. Pivoted messaging from "AI SDR" to "AI workforce platform."
- **Artisan ("Ava")** — billboarded "stop hiring humans"; ~$1.5-3K/mo per Ava. Founder claimed $5M ARR in 12 months; G2 reviews show heavy churn through 2025 and refund complaints. No independent ARR confirmation.
- **Harvey** — legal AI for BigLaw. **The cleanest of the cohort.** ~$50M ARR end-2024, $75M+ ARR cited in Dec 2025 press around $5B valuation. Logos confirmed via firm press releases (PwC, A&O Shearman, Ashurst, Macfarlanes). Explicitly **kept seat-based pricing** because elite legal won't accept algorithmic outcome attribution.
- **Hippocratic AI** — healthcare voice agents at ~$9/hr per agent vs ~$90/hr nurse cost. Pure FTE-replacement framing. Tens of millions in pilot revenue with Penn Medicine, WellSpan. Outcome data not published.
- **Lindy / CrewAI / MultiOn / /dev/agents** — horizontal platforms, hybrid usage+platform pricing. /dev/agents raised $56M seed at ~$500M (Index, CapitalG). CrewAI $18M Series A (Insight).
- **Klarity** — finance/contract review. Per-document + platform fee.
- **Replicant / Cresta** — voice agents in support; per-conversation/per-minute.
- **Adept** — effectively dissolved June 2024 reverse acqui-hire by Amazon. The canonical "AaaS company that died quietly."

### Pricing taxonomy — what's winning

- **Per-resolution / per-task** — winning in CX support (Sierra, Decagon, Replicant). Cost-per-resolution drops $8 (human) → $1-2 (agent).
- **Per-FTE-replaced** — Artisan, Hippocratic AI, 11x. Marketing-friendly but harder to defend at scale.
- **Per-outcome (booked meeting, qualified lead)** — emerging but hardest to attribute.
- **Hybrid (platform + usage / ACU credits)** — **dominant emerging pattern for developer-facing AaaS.** Cognition, CrewAI, Lindy.
- **Per-seat holdouts** — Harvey, GitHub Copilot, regulated verticals.

### Where AaaS is winning vs hype

**Winning:** CX support, voice in healthcare/insurance, structured legal review, bounded SWE tasks, chargeback recovery, healthcare scribing.

**Still vaporware:** full autonomous SWE on greenfield, full autonomous marketing/design, generalist computer-use agents (Claude Computer Use, OpenAI Operator demo well, narrow real-world reliability).

### Reality check (Gartner / Forrester / MIT)

- **Gartner (Jun 2025):** projects **>40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by end-2027** — citing escalating costs, unclear value, inadequate risk controls. Plummer called much of it "agent washing."
- **Forrester (Q3 2025 CIO survey):** **73% of agentic AI POCs from 2024 had not converted to production** by mid-2025. Top blockers: security review, integration cost, "unable to reproduce the demo."
- **MIT NANDA (Aug 2025):** widely circulated "95% of GenAI pilots fail to produce measurable revenue impact" finding.

## 3. Outcome-Driven Apps

### Definition

Feature-driven SaaS sells **access** (seats, modules). Outcome-driven apps sell **completed work** — a measurable, billable event the buyer would otherwise pay a human to produce. Three axes:

- **Unit sold:** resolved ticket, won chargeback, served demand letter, booked-and-held meeting, scribed encounter — not a login.
- **Measurement boundary:** the system event that triggers billing must be detectable without human judgment (ticket closes without escalation, calendar shows held meeting, processor confirms reversal).
- **Pricing structure:** per-event ($0.99/resolution, $300+/demand) or contingency (% of recovered amount). Almost always paired with minimum monthly commits and attribution windows.

Bloomberg projects subscription pricing falls from ~60% to ~30% of software over the next decade while outcome pricing climbs from ~10% toward ~60%. Reality check: only ~3% of vendors run *pure* outcome pricing today; ~65% of incumbents are **hybrid** (seat + outcome overlay).

### Named examples + pricing + logos

| Product | Outcome sold | Price | Notable logos |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intercom Fin** | Fin-resolved conversation | $0.99/resolution, 50/mo min | Anthropic, Lightspeed, Fundrise |
| **Zendesk AI Agents** | Automated resolution | $1.50 committed / $2.00 PAYG | (Resolution Platform launch) |
| **Sierra** | Resolved CX session | ~$1.50/resolution, $150K floor | Sonos, WeightWatchers, SiriusXM, ADT, Casper, Ramp |
| **Decagon** | Per conversation/resolution | Median $400K ACV | Eventbrite, Bilt, Notion, Duolingo |
| **Ada** | Resolution | Custom; ~$30K start, $70K median | Square, Verizon, Wealthsimple |
| **Crescendo** | Per-resolution all-inclusive | + Total Outcome Guarantee Oct 2025 | (PartnerHero acquired) |
| **Chargeflow** | Recovered chargeback | **25% of recovered amount**, no win = no fee | 20,000+ Shopify/SaaS merchants |
| **EvenUp** | Demand letter | All-in per-case, ~$300 base / $500-800 with add-ons | 1,000+ plaintiff firms |
| **Harvey** | (NOT outcome-priced) | Per-seat enterprise ~$100-200/seat/mo | PwC, A&O Shearman — counter-example |
| **Abridge** | Healthcare encounter scribed | Per-encounter | Penn-tier health systems |

### The attribution problem (where deals break)

- **Intercom Fin's solved version:** "Fin Resolved" only counts when conversation closes with **no human teammate involved**. Procedure handoff explicitly does not bill. Cleanest published methodology — they made the boundary binary.
- **Zendesk's painful lesson:** ambiguity over "resolution" produced customer disputes early; tightened to "AI agent fully resolves without human intervention."
- **Easy case:** chargeback recovery — the processor (Stripe, Adyen, Shopify Payments) emits a definitive win/loss event. Chargeflow only collects on the win.
- **Hard case ("ghost work"):** when a human nudges mid-flow, who owns the outcome? Most vendors resolve by *not* billing whenever a human touches it — conservative for the vendor, generous to the buyer.
- **Contractual hedges seen everywhere:** monthly minimums ($5K floor common), attribution windows (24-72h after close), reopen clauses (refund if reopened), dispute SLAs.

### Buyer migration — yes, real

- Bessemer's "labor budget = the new software budget" thesis is now baseline pitch language.
- a16z/Bessemer 2026 prediction: "CFOs replace users as the real buyers; agents stop being apps and start being employees with budgets, permissions, and outcome-based pricing."
- Vendor positioning shifted en masse to "digital worker" / "AI employee" framing — explicit move to land in labor P&L, not IT.
- McKinsey 2025 State of AI: only **39% of enterprises can attribute any EBIT impact to AI** — which is exactly why pure outcome contracts still fail at scale.

### Winning vs struggling

**Winning** (third-party-emitted billing event, high volume, replacing measurable labor): CX support, chargebacks, demand drafting, healthcare scribing, claims, KYC ops, AR collections.

**Struggling** (judgment-heavy, multi-actor, vendor or buyer can game the trigger): engineering/coding (Cursor, Copilot, Claude Code all pulled back to seat + usage; GitHub Copilot moving to usage credits Jun 2026), design (still seat — no agreed unit-of-outcome), elite legal (Harvey deliberately seat), pure SDR per-meeting (11x retreated), RevOps platforms (Clari, Gong — closed deal too multi-touch).

**CTO diagnostic question:** *who emits the billing event, and can either side game it?* If "the vendor's own system marks the outcome complete," demand audit logs, reopen clauses, dispute SLA in writing.

## 4. Embedded AI Analytics

### Definition (the boundary)

Embedded AI analytics = an analytics surface that lives **inside another product or workflow** and uses LLMs to translate natural-language intent into queries against a **governed data layer** (semantic layer + permissions), returning visualizations, metrics, or actions in-context.

| | Traditional BI (Tableau, Looker, Mode) | AI-Powered BI (Tableau Pulse, Looker Studio AI) | Embedded AI Analytics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where it lives | Standalone app | Standalone app | Inside another product |
| Construction | Pre-built by analyst | Pre-built + AI features bolted on | Generated at query time over semantic layer |
| Coverage | 10 dashboards = 10 use cases | Same | Long tail covered by NL+semantics |

Clean test: if AI is asking the user's customers questions inside the user's product → embedded. Inside Tableau → AI-powered BI. Without semantic layer or governance → generic chat-with-data, not the category.

### Vendor map (three tiers)

**AI-native workspace (data team + power user buyer)**
- **Sigma Computing / Sigma AI** — spreadsheet UX on cloud DW; "Ask Sigma" with editable, traceable steps. ~$5K-$50K typical ACV. SDK + white-label embeds. Snowflake-native sweet spot.
- **Hex / Hex Magic** — SQL+Python+notebook+visual cells with AI assist. Per-editor seat + compute. Free Community plan. Buyer: data scientists, analytics engineers.
- **Julius AI** — flat-rate ChatGPT-for-data; Pro from ~$20/mo. Connects to Snowflake/BigQuery/Postgres. Founder/ops/analyst buyer. Wedge: zero setup, time-to-first-chart in seconds.
- **Briefer (YC)** — Notion-like collaborative SQL+Python notebook with AI.

**Vertical / agentic**
- **Definite (definite.app)** — opinionated full data stack (warehouse + ELT + BI + AI) bundled for startups. Sells against the cost of assembling Fivetran+Snowflake+Looker.
- **Patterns.app** — pivoted to a deal-execution agent for finance/M&A firms (XLSX/PPT/PDF/data-room → comp tables, LBO models, IC memos). Vertical not horizontal.
- **Athenic AI** — natural-language agent over ERP/CRM/warehouse; $4.3M round Jan 2025 led by BMW i Ventures.
- **Glean** — Enterprise Graph (Sept 2025 third-gen Assistant); ARR doubled to ~$200M, $7.2B valuation. CIO buyer.

**Embed-SDK tier (sold to *other* SaaS companies)**
- **Luzmo** — embedded analytics SDK; Starter $495/mo; priced on monthly viewers + designers.
- **Embeddable** — dev-first React SDK.
- **Explo** — partial public pricing; SDK/iframe.
- **Quill** — NL-query-as-a-service for embedding.

**Semantic-layer infrastructure (picks-and-shovels)**
- **Cube.dev / Cube D3** — D3 launched Jun 2 2025 as "first agentic analytics platform built on universal semantic layer." 200+ companies, 500K+ lines of semantic code in 90 days post-GA. Named Leader in 2025 GigaOm Radar.

### The wedge against Tableau

- **Sigma:** spreadsheet UX on live cloud-warehouse + traceable AI steps. Reviewers: business users build dashboards on live Snowflake "in under an hour." Tableau requires an analyst.
- **Hex:** complex model built by data scientist becomes interactive app for business users **with one click** — collapses the analyst→consumer handoff Tableau/Looker enforces.
- **Julius:** zero setup. No semantic layer, no notebook config, no per-seat pricing. Time-to-first-chart in seconds.
- **Tableau Pulse:** pushes proactive digests; defensive posture, not category redefinition. Industry observation: "Tableau's recent updates have been entirely centered on AI initiatives like Pulse."

### Buyer migration

**Two tracks coexist:**
- Horizontal AI BI (Sigma, Hex, Looker) — still data/analytics-team buyer.
- New entrant tier (Julius, Definite, Athenic, Briefer + Mixpanel/Amplitude/PostHog) — **PM, ops, founder, CFO**. Athenic explicitly: "eliminates reliance on analysts." Definite: "BI tools for startups without a data team." PostHog: fully self-serve, no sales call.

**Strategic read:** the buyer didn't *replace* the data team — a new non-data buyer emerged for a different SKU. Two motions, often coexisting in the same company.

### Vertical AI analytics — the real second-order move

- **Drivetrain** — AI-native FP&A.
- **Mosaic** — FP&A; **acquired by Hibob Feb 2025** (FP&A consolidating into HRIS).
- **Causal** — formula-savvy modeling.
- **Runway** — design-forward FP&A.
- **Pry** — acquired by Brex (2023), distributed via Brex.
- **Patterns.app** — M&A deal-execution.

**Vertical wins where the workflow has a stable schema** (P&L, cap table, deal package). **Horizontal BI wins where the schema is custom.** Second-order effect: as vertical eats high-value workflows, horizontal BI gets pushed toward lower-value long-tail — exactly what NL agents are best at. Tableau/Looker get squeezed from both ends.

## 5. Hyper-Personalized Micro-SaaS

### What changed 2022 → 2026

- **Inference cost collapse.** GPT-4 March 2023 ~$30/$60 per M tokens → GPT-4o mini late 2024 ~$0.15/$0.60. A feature that cost $2 of inference per user-month flipped to ~$0.05. Gross margins flipped from negative to 80%+.
- **Eval/observability commoditized.** Braintrust, Langfuse (YC W23), Helicone made LLM observability a 30-min setup.
- **Vector DBs at $20/mo.** Pinecone Serverless (Jan 2024 consumption), Turbopuffer (Shopify alum, fractions of a cent/query), pgvector mature on Supabase free tier <1M vectors. RAG stopped being infra work.
- **Vertical fine-tunes / distillation.** OpenAI distillation Oct 2024 DevDay; Anthropic prompt caching expanded 2025; LoRA via Together/Fireworks <$50 to train.
- **Coding agents.** Cursor reportedly $200M+ ARR by mid-2025. Claude Code GA May 2025. Bolt.new $20M ARR in 2 months early 2025. Lovable $17M ARR in 3 months mid-2025. v0 (Vercel) similar arc. Compressed prototype-to-production from weeks to days.
- **Distribution shifts.** X build-in-public displaced ProductHunt as primary launch channel. Vertical Discords + Skool. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) replacing some classic SEO.

### Named solos / micro-teams with traction

**Disclosed publicly (with direction, not just snapshot):**
- **Pieter Levels (@levelsio)** — PhotoAI in $80-120K MRR range through 2025, **drifting down from ~$150K peak late 2023/early 2024**. InteriorAI ~$30-40K MRR. RemoteOK declining. *Direction matters: PhotoAI is the canonical wrapper that worked, and it has been flat-to-down.*
- **Marc Lou (@marc_louvion)** — ShipFast crossed $1M+ lifetime by 2024, **declined in 2025** as boilerplate market saturated.
- **Danny Postma** — sold HeadshotPro for "seven figures" (claimed, unverified) after ~$70K MRR solo. Now on the build-in-public circuit more than shipping.
- **Tony Dinh (@tdinh_me)** — TypingMind cumulative high six figures; growth slowed after ChatGPT added projects/memory.
- **Jon Yongfook (Bannerbear)** — ~$50K+ MRR, durable, growing slowly. **Notably *not* an LLM wrapper** — that's the point. API-first B2B image generation.

**Wrappers that became products (bigger, but show the pattern):**
- **Granola** — meeting notetaker; raised at ~$250M valuation 2025.
- **Cluely** — $3M ARR within months of mid-2025 launch.
- **Bolt.new, v0, Lovable** — full-stack vibe coding platforms.

### What "hyper-personalized" actually means

- **Per-user fine-tuned model.** PhotoAI literally trains a LoRA on your face — model-per-customer.
- **Vertical-specific agentic assistants.** "AI for Shopify owners," "AI bookkeeper for Etsy sellers," "AI SDR for solo founders." Narrow ICP where one prompt+workflow beats ChatGPT generality.
- **Tiny-market vertical models.** Chiropractor intake, dental scheduling, indie-game-dev playtest analyzers — sold cold-outbound at $200-2000/mo per seat, often <50 customers but $20-100K MRR.
- **Personal AI / second brain.** Granola (per-meeting context), Reflect, Mem (relaunched), Rewind/Limitless — personalization via your own data corpus.

### Distribution without ad spend

- **Audience-led launches** — Levels, Marc Lou, Tony Dinh: 100K+ X followers became the launch channel.
- **Programmatic SEO 2.0** — post-HCU survivors did fewer, deeper, real-utility pages, not LLM slop.
- **Reddit + niche Discords** — r/SideProject, r/Shopify, r/dentistry.
- **AppSumo lifetime deals** revived 2024-2025 for early MRR.
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** — getting cited by ChatGPT/Perplexity becoming a real channel.

### The wrapper graveyard (concrete kills)

- **Custom GPTs (late 2023)** killed dozens of single-prompt SaaS overnight.
- **Claude Artifacts + Projects** killed lightweight AI coding sandbox + document collab wrappers.
- **ChatGPT native image (4o, 2025)** crushed the lower tier of headshot/avatar generators. Survivors went vertical (wedding, real-estate, e-com product photography) where workflow is the moat.
- **Cursor + Claude Code** ate "AI pair programmer" wrappers and standalone "AI commit message" / "AI code review" tools.

### Solos still winning vs getting crushed

**Winning:** vertical SaaS with domain data (legal intake, dental, real-estate ops), creator tooling with built-in audience moat, B2B workflow tools where buyer doesn't care about the model, internal-ops "AI for [boring industry]."

**Crushed:** horizontal chat/UI wrappers, generic image/video gen, "AI for writers/marketers" without distribution moat, anything a foundation lab can ship as a feature in one quarter.

## 6. SMB / Solopreneur Playbook

### The "agency-shaped business" trend

Hybrid where AI does the bulk of labor, a thin human layer handles exceptions, customer buys an outcome. Backed by 8VC ("AI-native services"), a16z (the "AI rollup" thesis — buy fragmented service businesses, inject AI, expand margin 10% → 30%+), General Catalyst's Applied AI, Khosla, Greg Isenberg's "agency-as-a-product."

Named operators: 11x (agency replacement), Crescendo (CX-as-service), Cresta, Distyl AI (Schmidt-backed), Sierra, Decagon, Harvey, EvenUp, Abridge.

### 7 concrete moves SMBs/solos are making

1. **Vertical AI-native pivots** — away from horizontal "AI for SMBs" toward "AI for HVAC dispatchers," "AI for roofing estimators," "AI for dental insurance verification." ServiceTitan + FieldRoutes ecosystem solos textbook case.
2. **Per-outcome / usage pricing replacing per-seat** — 11x per meeting booked, Fin per resolution, EvenUp per demand letter.
3. **Service-first then productize** — sell as done-for-you for 6-12 months, productize once workflow is repeatable. Dan Mall, Jay Clouse, Greg Isenberg, Dickie Bush evangelizing.
4. **Owning workflow vs owning data** — solos targeting *workflow ownership* (the system of action) because foundation models commoditize *data interpretation*. Examples: Clay (GTM), Gumloop, Lindy, Relay.app.
5. **Platform-distribution leverage** — building on Shopify App Store, HubSpot, ServiceNow, Salesforce AppExchange, Intuit. Triple Whale / Gorgias / Klaviyo ecosystem solos report 50-70% of leads from app-store discovery.
6. **Audience-first launches** — ship to existing audience first; build-in-public replaced paid CAC. Levels, Marc Lou, Tony Dinh, Postma — near-zero paid spend.
7. **Hiring shift** — 5-10 person teams hiring "AI engineers" (eval pipelines, agent design, retrieval) over growth marketers.

### Where small builders are winning (2025-2026 evidence)

- **Vertical agentic in trades / back-office:** Trunk Tools (construction), Ascend (accounting back-office), Anterior (prior auth), Eve.legal (paralegal review, ~$400M valuation 2025).
- **Replacing entire labor categories:** Decagon/Sierra/Crescendo (tier-1 support), 11x/Artisan (basic SDR), Harvey/EvenUp/Eve (paralegal review), Abridge (~$2.75B valuation), Suki, Ambience Healthcare (~$1B).
- **Niche agents incumbents won't build:** vertical CRMs for unsexy industries — Mosaic (auto detailing), Jobber + AI ecosystem, Housecall Pro add-ons. Solos report $20-80K MRR within 12 months.

### Where small builders are getting crushed

- Model wrappers without proprietary workflow/data — ChatGPT/Claude shipping native PDF, Projects, MCP, file search killed thousands of "Chat with your X" startups.
- Horizontal "AI assistant for everything" — out-distributed by Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, ChatGPT Business.
- Foundation-model collisions — voice cloning (ElevenLabs), code completion (Cursor + Claude Code), Whisper killing transcription.
- Incumbent-bundled features — writing assistants vs Grammarly + Notion AI; meeting note-takers vs Zoom AI Companion / Teams Recap (free tier). Otter.ai growth slowed materially in 2025.

### Distribution: organic vs paid

- **Organic decisively winning for SMBs/solos.** Paid CAC up 20-40% YoY on Meta/Google as AI-generated ad supply floods auctions. LLM-driven search collapsing classic SEO traffic 15-40% for informational queries (Ahrefs, Similarweb 2025 data).
- **Working:** founder-led content (LinkedIn, X, YouTube), niche communities (Slack/Discord/Circle), podcast guesting, build-in-public loops, GEO.
- **Not working:** programmatic SEO content farms, broad Meta/Google paid for horizontal SaaS, cold outbound (deliverability collapsing 2025 post-Google/Yahoo sender rules + AI-spam saturation).
