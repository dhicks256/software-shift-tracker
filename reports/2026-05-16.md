# Software Shift Tracker — 2026-05-16

**Window:** 2026-05-02 → 2026-05-16. Two-week catch-up — the 2026-05-09 weekly run never fired (the scheduled routine was disabled by a repo-access failure), so this diff covers both weeks against the 2026-05-02 seed.

## What's new this week

- **Sierra** closed a **$950M round** (Tiger Global, GV) at a **$15B+ valuation**, disclosing **$150M ARR** as of February (May 4). The largest AaaS raise of the window — it resets the funding ceiling for the customer-service agent category.
- **EvenUp** launched **Pre-Litigation-as-a-Service (PLAAS)** (reported May 14) — pairing its legal AI with EvenUp's own U.S. case-management staff to run the full pre-litigation lifecycle, with >$10M in early subscriptions. A pure-software vendor crossing into managed services: selling the *settled case*, not the tool. This is the AaaS "sell the outcome" thesis bleeding into an established software vendor.
- **OpenAI and Anthropic each stood up PE-funded AI-consulting JVs** (May 5) — OpenAI's "Deployment Company" seeded ~$4B at a $10B pre-money; Anthropic's seeded $1.5B (Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman). The "AI-native McKinsey" / buy-services-inject-AI thesis going frontier-lab.
- **ServiceNow** used **Knowledge 2026** (May 5) to expand its **Autonomous Workforce** — four new AI-specialist categories across IT, CRM, employee services, and security; cites an L1 IT-desk specialist resolving cases "99% faster than human agents." An incumbent moving from advisory AI to AI that "senses, decides, and acts."
- **Gong** disclosed **ARR topped $500M** with growth **accelerating past 55% YoY** — its tenth straight quarter of accelerating growth (May 12). Counter-evidence that AI-native revenue tooling can accelerate *through* SaaS-multiple compression.

## What changed (watchlist movement)

- **Cursor** → moved **Bugbot from $40/seat to usage-based billing** (May 11, effective at renewals after June 8); shipped Cursor 3.3 with parallel agents (May 7). A concrete per-seat-pricing-death data point inside dev tools.
- **Sigma Computing** → shipped **"Agent Skills"** and the Call API action to GA (May 8) — letting AI assistants authenticate and create documents programmatically. An analytics platform building explicitly for the agent-as-user pattern.
- **Harvey** → named official legal-AI partner of the **NY Liberty** (May 4), part of a run of pro-sports sponsorships. A consumer-grade brand push — legal AI spending capital on mainstream credibility, not just pilots.
- **Hippocratic AI** → announced **six senior executive appointments** (May 14), including Chief Legal Officer, Chief Communications Officer, and an FDA-regulatory VP. A legal/comms/regulatory hiring cluster reads as credibility-hardening, not growth.
- **Lovable** → confirmed **$400M ARR** and instituted **automatic 10% annual raises** (May 7); 200 staff, targeting 400 by year-end.
