- **Will any public SaaS company file a disclosure attributing seat contraction to AI?** Still zero — Atlassian Q3 FY26 (May 2026) showed 32% revenue growth and NRR >120%, contradicting the "SaaSpocalypse" narrative. Data Center seat-expansion moderation is not a contraction disclosure.
- **Will outcome-pricing per-resolution floor move toward $0? STRUCTURAL SHIFT (Jun 15 2026):** Salesforce acquiring Fin (Intercom) for $3.6B collapses the independent outcome-pricing market. Salesforce now controls both Agentforce ($2/conv) and Fin ($0.99/resolution). Zendesk ($1.50/$2.00) is the last major independent. HubSpot ($0.50) is the low-cost outlier. Watch: whether Zendesk prices down or gets acquired next.
- **Cognition/Devin ARR — RESOLVED (May 27 2026):** $1B at $25B valuation; $492M ARR; enterprise logos: Mercedes-Benz, NASA, Goldman Sachs, Santander. Narrative thread closed.
- **Will 11x.ai recover or wind down?** New "Mike" AI phone rep product shipped; #1 in Ramp AI-native GTM spend (May 2026); IBM Agent Connect integration (Feb 2026). Signals recovery, not wind-down. No independent ARR confirmation yet.
- **Will Gartner's >40% agentic AI cancellation forecast track?** New signal: Gartner published "$234B enterprise SaaS spend at risk from agentic AI" (Jul 1 2026) — "agentic arbitrage" coined; 20% of enterprise app software at risk of displacement by 2030. Separate from the cancellation forecast but directionally consistent. IDC: global AI agents to surpass 1B by 2029. Forrester: seat-based pricing fell from 21% to 15% of SaaS market share in one year. Watch Q3+Q4 2026 analyst updates.
- **Will Microsoft Copilot move further into bundled M365 tiers? RESOLVED (Jul 1 2026):** M365 price increase live; 8–43% depending on plan; Copilot bundled into all base tiers with no opt-out. "AI tax" framing dominant in enterprise IT press. No hard churn data yet. Thread closed — now watch renewal-cycle churn data (most EA renewals in Q4 calendar 2026).
- **Will the AI rollup / "buy-services-inject-AI" thesis produce marquee vehicles?** Both vehicles now live and operating. OpenAI DeployCo: second acquisition (Northslope, Jul 8); OpenAI Presence launched Jul 22 (75% auto-resolution). "Ode with Anthropic" formally launched Jul 15; Baker Tilly first named client Jul 20. Watch: named client logos and ARR disclosure from both vehicles.

- **Will 11x.ai's "AI SDRs don't work" admission reshape the AI GTM category?** CEO Prabhav Jain on CNBC Jul 10: conceded the core product framing is broken. The company that popularized "AI SDR" is walking it back. Watch: whether peers (Artisan, Clay, Outreach AI) follow with similar admissions or defend the category.
- **Will Cursor's lead survive Claude Code maturity?** SpaceX announced $60B all-stock acquisition of Cursor/Anysphere (Jun 16). NOT closed — Q3 2026 expected close; HSR regulatory review underway.
