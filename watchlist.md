## Narrative threads to watch

- **Will any public SaaS company file a disclosure attributing seat contraction to AI?** Closest signal yet: Salesforce reduced customer support headcount from ~9K to ~5K in 2026, operationally attributed to Agentforce absorbing workload. Not a formal SEC filing, but the first public Tier-1 vendor statement of this kind. HubSpot Q2 NRR of 102% and net new ARR guided below CC revenue growth is a softer signal. Watch Salesforce Q3 FY27 for whether support contraction surfaces in formal disclosure language.
- **Will outcome-pricing per-resolution floor move toward $0?** HubSpot moved to $0.50 (April 14 2026); Intercom $0.99 and Zendesk $1.50–$2.00 held flat through May 23. HubSpot undercut sets new market floor — watch for incumbent response.
- **Cognition/Devin ARR — RESOLVED (May 27 2026):** $1B at $25B valuation; $492M ARR; enterprise logos: Mercedes-Benz, NASA, Goldman Sachs, Santander. Narrative thread closed.
- **Will 11x.ai recover or wind down?** New "Mike" AI phone rep product shipped; #1 in Ramp AI-native GTM spend (May 2026); IBM Agent Connect integration (Feb 2026). Signals recovery, not wind-down. No independent ARR confirmation yet.
- **Will Gartner's >40% agentic AI cancellation forecast track?** No new analyst follow-up in-window; watch IDC/Forrester Q3+Q4 2026.
- **Will Microsoft Copilot move further into bundled M365 tiers?** July 1 2026 price increase confirmed and took effect; Copilot Business held at $21/seat; bundle prices increased ~5–7%. Customers who locked in 3-year terms before June 30 hold prior pricing. Churn vs. lock-in data not yet publicly surfaced.
- **Will the AI rollup / "buy-services-inject-AI" thesis produce marquee vehicles?** OpenAI Deployment Company formally launched May 11 2026 ($4B, TPG-led, Tomoro acquisition, ~150 FDEs from day one). Anthropic JV (Blackstone/Goldman, $1.5B) confirmed May 4. Both vehicles are live.
- **Will GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) develop measurable attribution?** Narrative-level only; no discrete in-window event.
- **Cursor acquired by SpaceX — RESOLVED (Aug 14 2026):** $60B all-stock deal closed; Cursor folded into SpaceXAI. OpenAI intends to wind down model access November 12 citing prior TOS violations. Claude Code and GitHub Copilot are the primary beneficiaries of the resulting developer uncertainty. Narrative thread shifts to: will enterprise Cursor users migrate before the OpenAI cutoff?
